How it works

  1. 1

    Paste your site

    Drop in your homepage URL. Overheard reads it and asks Claude to build a relevance rubric — what the problem is, who has it, and what to look for.

  2. 2

    It listens and filters

    A scheduled job searches Reddit (and X if you add credentials) with your keywords. Every candidate goes through an AI intent filter — only genuine help-seekers make the cut.

  3. 3

    You get a clean digest

    Each morning (or whenever you run it), you get a short list: what they said, why it's relevant, and a suggested angle if you decide to reach out. Your team decides — Overheard never posts.
