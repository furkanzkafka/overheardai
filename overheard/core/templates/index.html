How it works

Three quiet steps between you and the right conversation.

01

Paste your site.

Drop in your homepage. Overheard reads it and asks Claude to draft a relevance rubric — what the problem is, who has it, and the signals of a genuine need. You review it before anything goes live.

02

It listens, then filters.

A scheduled job casts a wide keyword net across Reddit & X. Every candidate runs through an AI intent filter that scores it against your rubric — so only real help-seekers make the cut, not idle chatter.

03

You get a morning digest.

Each morning, a short list lands in your inbox: what they said, why it's a fit, and a suggested angle if you choose to reach out. Mark replied, ignore, move on. Overheard never posts on your behalf.

👀

Overheard never posts, DMs, or auto-replies to anyone. It's a read-only listener. What you do with what you hear is entirely up to you.
